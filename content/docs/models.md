## Descriptions

- **DYAMOND 3**: DYAMOND-3 +extensions (based on the existing 5km 1 year AMIP-2020 from the 2025 hackathon):
- **DYAMOND 3 SST+4K**: DYAMOND-3 run with SST+4K ('Cess Type')
- **Coupled**: Any Coupled km-scale atmosphere and ocean (note length and specifics)
- **ECOMIP**: Aug-Sept 2024, 2 day cases and/or 2 months (Following ECOMIP Protocol)
- **1km or sub 1km**: Anything at ~1km scale (or less), short is fine, regional or regionally refined is fine
- **Initialized**: Single or series of DYAMOND1/2 type 40 day experiments
- **Idealized**: Aquaplanet with possibly RCE, different resolutions (10,5,2.5km) and RCE
- Please note length of simulations, and resolutions
- Multi-resolution is welcomed for any of the simulations (please note it)
