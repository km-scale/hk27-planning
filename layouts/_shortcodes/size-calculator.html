Cells per field: $12\cdot4^z$ × domain fraction (column Domain; ocean ≈ 0.7, land ≈ 0.3). Values per time step: #2D + #3D × #levels. Time steps: years / interval (unit h, d or mo) (1 year = 365.25 days, 1 month = 1/12 year). A mean stream at interval $\tau_\mathrm{m}$ is also aggregated to every checked hierarchy step coarser than $\tau_\mathrm{m}$. An inst stream at $\tau_\mathrm{i}$ must also be available as means at $\tau_\mathrm{i}$ and at every checked step coarser than it. If its variables are already part of a mean stream, select that stream under Means in: only the mean intervals it does not provide are added. Hover a row for its intervals.
